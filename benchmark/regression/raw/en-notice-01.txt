PENNERBRIDGE WATER
PUBLIC NOTICE OF ESSENTIAL WATER MAIN REPLACEMENT
Hollowgate Lane and Marsden Row, Ashcombe Green

Notice reference: PW/STW/2026/04872
Street works permit: 0492-HG-26-117, issued under the New Roads and Street Works Act 1991
Scheme code: TRUNK-RENEW-38

Works will begin on Monday 5 October 2026 and are expected to be complete by Friday 18 December 2026, a duration of eleven weeks. We are replacing 1,240 metres of cast iron water main, much of it laid in 1934, with new 180 mm ductile iron pipe. The existing main has suffered nine bursts in the last three years and the replacement will improve water pressure and reliability for around 2,700 properties in the Ashcombe Green and Pellow Hill areas.

Working hours will be 07:30 to 17:30 Monday to Friday and 08:00 to 13:00 on Saturdays. There will be no working on Sundays or bank holidays. Noisy operations, including breaking out of the carriageway, will not start before 08:00.

What to expect
- Hollowgate Lane will be reduced to a single lane under three way temporary traffic signals between the junction with Marsden Row and the railway bridge.
- Marsden Row will be closed to through traffic from 12 October for approximately four weeks. A signed diversion will operate via Carlow Street and Dunmoor Avenue.
- Bus services 26 and 26A will be diverted away from Marsden Row for the duration of the closure. The stops at Marsden Row (stop codes 24118 and 24119) will be suspended and the nearest alternative stops are on Carlow Street.
- Parking restrictions will apply on both streets. Residents holding a permit for Zone C will be able to use the temporary bays marked on Dunmoor Avenue.
- Access for residents, deliveries and emergency vehicles will be maintained at all times, although you may be asked to wait for a short period.
- Short interruptions to your water supply may be necessary when we connect the new main. We will give at least 48 hours written notice of any planned interruption. If your supply is interrupted without notice, please call our 24 hour emergency line.

Following the discoloured water that may occur after reconnection, run your cold kitchen tap gently for a few minutes until it clears. This is not harmful but we would ask you to avoid using washing machines until the water runs clear.

The site agent for these works is Derek Callaghan, who can be found at the site compound on the corner of Hollowgate Lane during working hours. General enquiries about the scheme should be made to our customer contact team quoting the notice reference above.

We apologise for the disruption these essential works will cause and thank you for your patience.

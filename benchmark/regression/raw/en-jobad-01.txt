The role
Clinical Engineering maintains an asset base of just over 9,400 medical devices across acute and community services. We are looking for a Senior Medical Engineering Technician to join the acute workshop team and take day to day responsibility for the infusion and respiratory device portfolio, which accounts for approximately 2,600 assets.

You will carry out planned preventative maintenance, fault diagnosis, repair and acceptance testing on a broad range of equipment, including volumetric and syringe infusion pumps, ventilators, patient monitors, defibrillators and theatre equipment. Electrical safety testing is performed to IEC 62353, and you will be expected to work to the departmental quality system, which is accredited to ISO 9001:2015 and ISO 13485:2016.

Main duties
1. Deliver the annual PPM schedule for your portfolio, maintaining a first time completion rate of at least 95 percent.
2. Investigate and repair reported faults, escalating any device incident that may be reportable to the MHRA within one working day.
3. Maintain accurate asset records in the department's equipment management database, including serial numbers, software versions and calibration certificates.
4. Supervise two Band 4 Medical Engineering Assistants and contribute to the training of apprentices on the Level 3 Engineering Technician standard.
5. Support the equipment library, including the decontamination and issue of loan devices.
6. Participate in the 1 in 6 on-call rota providing out of hours technical cover to critical care and theatres.

Person specification
Essential: HNC or HND in Electrical and Electronic Engineering or an equivalent qualification; a minimum of three years post qualification experience in a healthcare technical environment; demonstrable knowledge of medical device regulation and of IEC 60601-1; a full driving licence valid in the United Kingdom.
Desirable: registration as an Engineering Technician with the Engineering Council; manufacturer training on infusion or ventilation platforms; experience of a computerised maintenance management system.

What we offer
27 days annual leave rising to 33 days with service, plus eight bank holidays. Access to the NHS Pension Scheme. A structured continuing professional development allowance of GBP 800 per person per year. Free on site parking at the Thornlow Road site and a cycle to work scheme.

7. STANDARD OF SERVICE
7.1 The Supplier shall perform the Services with the reasonable care and skill required by section 13 of the Supply of Goods and Services Act 1982 and in accordance with the maintenance regime set out in Schedule 1, Part B.
7.2 All electrical work forming part of the Services shall comply with BS 7671:2018+A2:2022 and shall be certified on the forms specified at paragraph 4.3 of Annex C. Work on fixed gas appliances shall be undertaken only by operatives holding current ACS accreditation.
7.3 The Supplier maintains a quality management system certified to BS EN ISO 9001:2015 under certificate Q-118344 and an environmental management system certified to BS EN ISO 14001:2015. The Customer may request evidence of continued certification once in each Contract Year.

8. RESPONSE TIMES AND SERVICE CREDITS
8.1 Priority 1 (loss of heating, power or water to an occupied area): attendance within 4 working hours of the fault being logged on the helpdesk.
8.2 Priority 2 (fault affecting amenity but not occupation): attendance within 1 working day. Priority 3 (all other faults): attendance within 5 working days.
8.3 Where the attendance times at clauses 8.1 to 8.2 are not met, the Customer may claim a service credit of 2.5% of the monthly charge for each failure, capped at 15% of the monthly charge in any one month. Service credits are the Customer's exclusive financial remedy for delayed attendance.

9. CHARGES AND PAYMENT
9.1 The Annual Charge for the first Contract Year is £84,600 exclusive of VAT, invoiced monthly in arrears at £7,050.
9.2 Valid invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date. Sums unpaid after that date attract interest and compensation under the Late Payment of Commercial Debts (Interest) Act 1998.
9.3 Rates are held firm for 12 months and are thereafter indexed annually to the Consumer Prices Index (ONS series D7BT), subject to a ceiling of 4% in any year.
9.4 Labour outside core hours is charged at £68.00 per hour between 18:00 and 08:00 Monday to Friday and £92.50 per hour on Saturdays. Materials are charged at cost plus 12%.

10. HEALTH, SAFETY AND ACCESS
10.1 The Supplier shall discharge its duties under the Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 and the Control of Substances Hazardous to Health Regulations 2002, and shall lodge current COSHH assessments with the Customer before first attendance.
10.2 The Customer shall procure access to plant rooms and risers on reasonable notice. Hot works require a permit issued by the Head of Facilities or nominated deputy, countersigned by the Duty Manager of the affected building.
10.3 Waste electrical and electronic equipment removed under this Contract is consigned under WEEE producer registration WEE/JB3392AC and evidence of disposal is provided quarterly to Procurement.

11. LIABILITY
11.1 Nothing in this Contract limits either party's liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence or for fraudulent misrepresentation.
11.2 Subject to clause 11.1, the Supplier's total liability arising in any Contract Year, whether in contract, tort or otherwise, is limited to 125% of the Annual Charge for that year.
11.3 Clause 11.2 does not affect any right of the Customer under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 where the Customer contracts as a consumer.
